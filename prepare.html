Prepare for Your Diagnostic Scan

Knowing what to expect before, during, and after your imaging appointment ensures precision diagnostics, minimizes pre-exam apprehension, and provides a smooth clinical journey from check-in to digital report release[cite: 5].

Step 01

Prior Documentation

Bring your active prescription script from your doctor, your health insurance cards, and a government-issued photo ID. If available, bring comparative films or CDs from past examinations[cite: 5].

Step 02

Clothing & Metal Safety

Wear loose apparel free of metal buttons, zippers, or snaps. For MRI scans, all jewelry, watches, hearing aids, and metallic items must be stored securely in our private secure lockers[cite: 5].

Step 03

Fasting & Medications

Certain modalities like abdominal CT scans or contrast-enhanced evaluations require fasting for 4 to 6 hours. Continue taking regular prescription medications with small sips of water unless instructed otherwise[cite: 5].

Modality-Specific Safety Clearances

MRI Metal Screening

Patients with pacemakers, neurostimulators, cochlear implants, or certain metallic shrapnel must notify our staff immediately. Our safety team conducts a comprehensive screening questionnaire before entry[cite: 5].

CT Contrast Dye Guidelines

If your test involves intravenous (IV) contrast dye, inform coordinators if you have a history of severe allergies, iodine reactions, asthma, or impaired kidney function so we can customize your protocol safely[cite: 5].
