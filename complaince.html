HIPAA Compliance & Patient Rights

Aura Imaging is deeply committed to protecting your medical privacy rights and maintaining absolute compliance with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA)[cite: 6].

Notice of Privacy Practices

This notice describes how medical information about you may be used and disclosed and how you can get access to this information[cite: 6]. Please review it thoroughly[cite: 6]. We are legally required by federal law to maintain the privacy of your Protected Health Information (PHI)[cite: 6].

Your Patient Rights Under HIPAA

As a patient at Aura Imaging, you maintain explicit control and access rights over your health records[cite: 6]:

  • Right to Inspect and Copy: You can request and review digital copies of your diagnostic MRI, CT, ultrasound, or X-ray reports and imaging files[cite: 6].
  • Right to Request Restrictions: You have the right to request limitations on certain uses or disclosures of your protected health data[cite: 6].
  • Right to Confidential Communications: You may request that we communicate with you regarding your medical matters in a specific alternative location or manner[cite: 6].
  • Right to an Accounting of Disclosures: You can request a record of instances where we have shared your health data outside clinical necessity[cite: 6].

Filing a Privacy or Compliance Complaint

If you believe your privacy rights have been violated, you may file a formal grievance directly with our internal compliance department or with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services[cite: 6]. We guarantee that filing a complaint will never result in retaliation or affect the quality of your clinical care[cite: 6].
