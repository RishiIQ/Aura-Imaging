Clinical Modalities

Advanced Diagnostic Services

Aura Imaging deploys next-generation scanning hardware integrated with AI-assisted software algorithms, delivering unmatched anatomical resolution, faster exam times, and patient-centered safety standards.

● High-Field Magnetic Resonance

3T Wide-Bore MRI

Our high-field 3T magnetic resonance imaging systems deliver superior spatial resolution for soft tissues, neurological structures, and musculoskeletal evaluations. Built with an expanded wide-bore architecture to dramatically minimize claustrophobia and lower acoustic scanning noise.

Applications

Brain, spine, neurological tracts, cartilage, and sports injuries.

Patient Comfort

Wider tunnel opening, ambient lighting, and ear-defense sound suppression.

Schedule 3T MRI Scan
● Ultra-Fast Computed Tomography

128-Slice CT Scan

Lightning-fast computed tomography offering high-definition 3D rendering of internal organs, vasculature, and skeletal structures. Integrated with advanced dose-modulation software to maintain strict patient radiation safety standards.

Applications

Cardiac CT, coronary scoring, pulmonary angiography, and trauma screening.

Safety Profile

Iterative AI reconstruction ensuring minimal radiation exposure.

Schedule CT Scan
● Real-Time Sonography

High-Resolution Ultrasound

Non-invasive diagnostic sonography utilizing high-frequency sound waves to generate real-time moving images of soft tissues, blood vessels, and internal organs with zero ionizing radiation exposure.

Applications

Vascular Doppler flow, abdominal scanning, pelvic, and obstetric evaluations.

Patient Experience

Painless, fast procedure with immediate specialist review.

Schedule Ultrasound
● Digital Radiography

Digital X-Ray Systems

Instantaneous digital X-ray imaging designed to evaluate skeletal structures, bone fractures, and thoracic conditions with maximum image sharpness and minimal radiation exposure time.

Applications

Skeletal trauma, orthopedic evaluations, and chest radiography.

Workflow

Walk-in friendly with script; images available to physicians instantly.
