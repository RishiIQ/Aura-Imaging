Legal & Compliance

Privacy Policy & HIPAA Guidelines

At Aura Imaging, safeguarding your Protected Health Information (PHI) and personal data is our highest priority. This policy outlines our data collection, security protocols, and compliance standards.

1. Commitment to HIPAA Compliance

Aura Imaging strictly adheres to the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA). All medical records, diagnostic scans, physician referral notes, and billing statements are encrypted using advanced security protocols both in transit and at rest. Access to patient records is strictly limited to authorized clinical and administrative personnel involved directly in your care.

2. Information We Collect

In order to provide accurate diagnostic imaging and coordinate with your referring physicians, we collect:

  • Personal identification details (full name, date of birth, government-issued photo ID).
  • Contact information (phone number, home address, email address).
  • Medical history, diagnostic requisitions, and physician notes.
  • Health insurance provider details, policy numbers, and billing data.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is utilized solely for clinical and operational excellence, including:

  • Scheduling and preparing patients for diagnostic scans (MRI, CT, Ultrasound, X-Ray).
  • Transmitting diagnostic reports securely to your primary care physician or specialist.
  • Processing insurance pre-authorizations and managing billing inquiries.
  • Improving patient safety, clinic workflows, and digital portal experiences.

4. Data Security & Encryption

We deploy robust administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, data alteration, or disclosure. Our online systems use secure SSL/TLS encryption for all digital form submissions, appointment requests, and portal logins.

5. Contact Our Privacy Officer

If you have questions regarding our privacy practices, wish to inspect your medical records, or want to report a compliance concern, please reach out to our institutional privacy team:
